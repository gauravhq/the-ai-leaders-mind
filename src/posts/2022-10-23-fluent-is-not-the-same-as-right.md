---
title: "Fluent Is Not the Same as Right"
date: 2022-10-23
pageDescription: "A model is fluent by default. Treat that fluency as a style, not a verdict."
---
A model speaks in complete, confident sentences whether it is right or wrong. Fluency is not something it earns on a given answer; it is the house style, applied evenly to the true and the mistaken.

Your brain did not evolve for this. For all of human history, someone who spoke clearly and without hesitation usually knew what they were talking about. We learned to read fluency as a signal of competence, and the machine trips that wire every time.

So separate the two on purpose. How well an answer is written tells you nothing about whether it is correct. A polished paragraph can be built on a wrong number, and it will not flinch.

The move is to test the substance and ignore the delivery. Ask what the claim rests on, what would make it false, what the model cannot see. Judge the content, not the confidence. The most dangerous answers are the ones that sound too good to question.
