---
title: "The Handoff Moment"
date: 2022-12-25
pageDescription: "Name the instant you stop thinking and start accepting."
---
There is a precise moment in working with a tool when your role changes. Up to that point you are thinking. After it you are accepting. The handoff is quiet, and most people never notice it happen.

It is worth learning to feel; it is the instant your own reasoning goes quiet and the model's answer becomes the plan. Sometimes that is fine, when the stakes are low and the tool is reliable. Sometimes it is the exact moment a bad decision gets made, because you handed off a call you should have kept.

You cannot manage a handoff you cannot feel. So practice noticing it. Catch yourself in the small relief of letting the answer stand. Ask, in that instant, whether this was a call to delegate or one to own.

The skill is not refusing to hand off. You have to, constantly, or nothing gets done. The skill is doing it on purpose, knowing which decisions you are keeping and which you are letting go.
