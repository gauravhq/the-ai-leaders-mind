---
title: "Name Your Default Bias"
date: 2023-03-12
pageDescription: "Everyone leans one way. Knowing yours is half the defense."
---
Bias is not a flaw in other people; it is the standard equipment everyone carries, and the useful question is not whether you have biases but which ones you lean on hardest.

Some people default to trusting the confident answer, because deferring feels efficient. Some anchor hard on the first number they see. Some chase the vivid example and ignore the boring base rate. Some look for what fits and file away what does not. Each person has a favorite, a groove their thinking slides into under pressure.

Knowing yours is most of the defense. A bias you can name, you can watch for. The one that runs you is the one you have never looked at directly, because it does not feel like bias from the inside. It feels like being right.

So do the honest work of naming your default. Look back at decisions that went wrong and find the pattern. Then, on the next hard call with a machine involved, you know which of your own reflexes to distrust first. That is not weakness. It is the beginning of judgment.
