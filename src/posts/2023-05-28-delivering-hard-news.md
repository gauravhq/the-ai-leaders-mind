---
title: "Delivering Hard News"
date: 2023-05-28
pageDescription: "The tool can draft it. Only you can carry it."
---
Hard news is a test of leadership that no tool can take for you. A layoff, a rejection, a serious mistake owned out loud. The machine can help you find the words. It cannot be the one who says them, and it should not be.

The temptation is real, because delivering hard news is uncomfortable and the tool makes discomfort easy to avoid. Let it write the message, send it through a channel, keep a screen between you and the person's reaction. Efficient, and a small abandonment.

What the person needs in that moment is not information. They have the information. They need a human being to be accountable, present, and honest with them; that is the whole point of the interaction, and it is exactly the part a machine cannot supply.

So use the tool to prepare, then show up yourself. Say the hard thing in your own voice, take the reaction, answer the questions. It costs you something, which is the point. The willingness to carry hard news personally is a large part of what makes people trust that you will lead them well through easier things too.
