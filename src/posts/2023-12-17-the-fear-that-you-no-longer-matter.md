---
title: "The Fear That You No Longer Matter"
date: 2023-12-17
pageDescription: "Not fear of losing the job, but fear that the thing you were good at stopped counting."
---
There is a fear deeper than losing a job, and it gets missed because it is quieter. It is the fear that the thing you were good at, the skill you built a career and an identity on, no longer matters. The job might survive. The pride in it might not.

This is grief as much as fear. When a machine does, in seconds, the thing you spent years mastering, the mastery does not feel like an asset anymore. It feels like a monument to a world that ended. And no amount of talk about productivity touches that, because the wound is about meaning, not output.

Leaders who miss this try to solve a meaning problem with efficiency arguments, and it never lands. What helps is helping the person find where their value moved. The old skill may matter less. The judgment, the experience, the pattern sense underneath it usually matter more, if someone helps them see it.

So take this fear seriously as the loss it is. Do not rush to reassure. Help the person redraw the line around what they are for, so the mastery they built becomes a foundation for what is next, rather than a headstone for what is gone.
