---
title: "The Person Who Will Not Touch the Tool"
date: 2024-01-28
pageDescription: "Stubbornness is usually a feeling in disguise. Find the feeling."
---
Every rollout has one. The person who simply will not engage with the new tool, who finds reasons, who drags. It is easy to file them as stubborn and push harder. That almost always makes it worse, because stubbornness is rarely the real thing. It is a feeling wearing a disguise.

Behind the refusal is usually a fear, and the refusals differ by which one. Fear of being replaced looks like hostility. Fear of looking incompetent looks like disinterest. Fear of losing control looks like endless objections about reliability. The behavior is the same shrug, and the cause underneath is specific.

So instead of overriding the refusal, get curious about it. Ask, and actually listen. What is it about this that does not sit right? The answer usually reveals the feeling, and the feeling is what you can actually address. You cannot argue someone out of a fear you have not named, and stubbornness is the name people use for a fear they have not been asked about.

Meet the person, find the feeling, address that. The refusal was never really about the tool. It was about something the person has not yet felt safe enough to say.
