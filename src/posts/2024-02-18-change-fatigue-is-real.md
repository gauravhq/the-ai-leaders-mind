---
title: "Change Fatigue Is Real"
date: 2024-02-18
pageDescription: "People asked to change too often stop trusting that any of it will last."
---
There is a limit to how much change people can absorb before they stop believing in it. Push too many transformations too fast, and people do not become more adaptable. They become exhausted and cynical, and they start quietly waiting for each new thing to pass, because so many did.

AI is landing on organizations that have often already spent their change budget on the last several initiatives. To the team, it can look like one more thing leadership is excited about this quarter, and the rational response to a history of passing enthusiasms is to conserve energy and not fully engage.

Ignoring this fatigue and pushing harder just deepens it. What helps is acknowledging it honestly, being clear about what is actually different this time and why it will stick, and not dressing up every small tool as a revolution.

Respect the fatigue as real information about your organization's history, not as resistance to overcome. People who have been through too many changes are not being difficult when they hold back. They are protecting themselves from disappointment they have earned the right to expect. Give them a reason to believe this one is worth the energy, and mean it.
