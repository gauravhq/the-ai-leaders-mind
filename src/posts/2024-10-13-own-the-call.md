---
title: "Own the Call"
date: 2024-10-13
pageDescription: "The final layer is a person putting their name on the decision."
---
Every good decision with a machine ends the same way, with a person owning the call. The final layer is not another analysis. It is someone putting their name on the outcome and being willing to answer for it. This is what turns a process into a decision.

Ownership is not a formality. It changes how the whole decision was made, because a person who knows they will own the result attends to it differently all the way through. They question the tool more honestly, weigh the human factors more carefully, and refuse to hide behind the process, because there is nowhere to hide when the call is explicitly theirs.

The tool cannot do this layer. It can inform every step and it can never own the outcome, because it cannot be answerable. The moment a decision has no human owner, it has no one whose judgment and reputation are on the line, and decisions with no one on the line get made carelessly.

So end every consequential decision with a clear owner. Not the model, not the process, not the room. A person who says, this is my call, and I will answer for it. That ownership is the last layer, and it is the one that holds all the others accountable, because it is the point where the decision stops being an output and becomes a human act.
