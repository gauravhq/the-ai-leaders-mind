title: "The Five Fears"
date: 2025-01-25
pageDescription: "Resistance to AI is usually fear, and the fear is more specific than it looks."
---
When people resist AI, we call them stubborn. Usually they are afraid, and the fear is more specific than it looks.

Underneath most resistance sits one of five fears. Fear of being replaced. Fear of looking incompetent at something new. Fear of losing control over work you were proud of. Fear that the thing you were good at no longer matters. And fear of a future you cannot picture yourself in. Name them and the resistance stops looking irrational. It looks human.

The mistake leaders make is arguing with the surface. They answer stubbornness with a better slide deck about productivity, and wonder why nothing moves. You cannot reason someone out of a fear you have not named.

Start by figuring out which fear you are actually facing, in yourself and in your team. The person who will not touch the tool because they fear being replaced needs something completely different from the one who is afraid of looking foolish. Address the real fear and the resistance often melts. Address the wrong one and you are just adding noise.
