---
title: "Algorithmic Empathy"
date: 2025-02-15
pageDescription: "A model can scale a message. It cannot scale you, and some rooms need you."
---
A model can now read the sentiment in a message, flag the customer who sounds upset, and draft a warm reply in seconds. Call it algorithmic empathy. It is genuinely useful, and it is not the real thing.

The machine detects the pattern of an emotion. It does not feel it, and it does not care. That difference is invisible on a good day and enormous on a hard one. When someone is grieving, or frightened, or quietly humiliated, they can tell the difference between being processed and being met.

The temptation for a busy leader is to let the tool carry the emotional load. Let it handle the note, the check-in, the difficult message. Efficient, and hollow.

Use algorithmic empathy for reach, not for presence. Let it help you notice who needs you and draft the routine warmth. Then show up yourself for the moments that decide whether a person feels seen. The machine can scale a message. It cannot scale you, and there are rooms where only you will do.
