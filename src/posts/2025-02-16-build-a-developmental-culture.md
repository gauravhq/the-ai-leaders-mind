---
title: "Build a Developmental Culture"
date: 2025-02-16
pageDescription: "A team that keeps growing beside the machine needs an environment designed for it."
---
Individual growth is good. A culture that produces growth across a team is leadership, and it does not happen by accident. A developmental culture, one where people keep sharpening their judgment rather than letting the tools do their thinking, has to be built and protected, especially against the quiet pull of convenience.

The pull is real. Every tool invites people to do less thinking, and a team left to default settings will slowly hand more of its judgment to the machines, feeling more productive while getting less capable. A developmental culture pushes against that, deliberately, by valuing and rewarding the things that keep people growing.

It looks like specific choices. Making it normal to form your own view before consulting the tool. Rewarding people who question outputs rather than just accepting them. Giving people problems to work through rather than answers to apply. Treating being a beginner as a normal part of work rather than a failure.

So build the environment on purpose. A team's default trajectory, beside capable tools, is toward comfortable dependence. A developmental culture bends that trajectory back toward growth, and it is one of the more valuable things a leader can create, because it determines whether your people become sharper alongside the machines or slowly, pleasantly, less able.
