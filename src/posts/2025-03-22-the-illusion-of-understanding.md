---
title: "The Illusion of Understanding"
date: 2025-03-22
pageDescription: "A clear explanation from the model can feel like your own understanding. It is not."
---
A good model can explain almost anything clearly, and clear explanations feel like understanding. You read the tidy summary, it makes sense, and you walk away believing you grasp the thing. Often you grasp the summary, not the thing.

It matters because understanding is what lets you catch an error. If you truly follow why a recommendation holds, you can feel it when a piece is wrong. If you only followed the explanation, you have no independent footing. You will nod along with a mistake as easily as with the truth.

The test is whether you can rebuild the reasoning without the model in front of you. Explain it to someone else. Predict what changes if a key fact changes. If you cannot, you borrowed the understanding, and borrowed understanding cannot check anything.

Use the model's explanations, but treat the warm feeling of clarity with suspicion. Real understanding survives the tool being switched off. The illusion of it disappears the moment you have to stand on your own.
