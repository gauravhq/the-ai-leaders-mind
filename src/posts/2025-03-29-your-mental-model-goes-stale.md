---
title: "Your Mental Model Goes Stale"
date: 2025-03-29
pageDescription: "The tool keeps changing. Your picture of what it can and cannot do quietly does not."
---
You form a picture of a tool early; it is good at this, weak at that, trust it here, check it there. That picture is useful, and it goes out of date faster than you update it.

The systems change under you. A weakness you learned to work around gets fixed, and you keep working around a problem that is gone. A limit you never hit becomes real as the tool takes on more, and you walk straight into it because your old map said the road was clear.

The result is a leader steering by a model of the machine that no longer matches the machine. It feels like knowledge. It is memory.

So refresh the picture on purpose. Now and then, test the tool at the edges of what you assume it can do. Where has it improved? Where has it started failing? Treat your sense of its strengths and weaknesses as something to re-earn, not a fact you filed away once and can trust forever.
