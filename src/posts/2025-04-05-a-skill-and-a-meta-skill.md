---
title: "A Skill and a Meta-Skill"
date: 2025-04-05
pageDescription: "Skills age out. The ability to keep learning compounds."
---
A skill is knowing how to use today's tool. A meta-skill is knowing how to keep learning as the tools keep changing. Only one of them is worth building a career on.

The specific model you mastered this year will be gone in a few. If your value is tied to it, your value has an expiration date. The people who stay useful across the shifts are the ones who got good at adapting itself, at picking up the next thing, at holding their expertise lightly enough to update it.

This is a different kind of learning. It is less about mastering a feature set and more about staying curious, noticing when your mental model is out of date, and being willing to be a beginner again on purpose.

The comfortable move is to go deep on the current tool and call it done. The durable move is to treat every tool as temporary and your ability to learn as the real asset. Skills age out. The meta-skill compounds. In a field that reinvents itself every couple of years, that is the only ground stable enough to stand on.
