---
title: "What Attention Costs Now"
date: 2025-04-12
pageDescription: "The scarce resource is your judgment. Spend it where it actually counts."
---
When a machine can do more of the work, the bottleneck moves; it is no longer how much you can produce. It is how well you can judge what the machine produces. Your attention becomes the scarce thing, and where you spend it decides your value.

Spent badly, it goes to the easy and the visible. You review what is quick to review and wave through what is hard, which is exactly backward, because the hard calls are the ones that needed you.

Spent well, attention goes to the few decisions that carry real weight and the few places the model is likely to be wrong. You let the routine flow and you slow down where a mistake would cost the most.

This is a skill worth naming and building. Ask, every so often, whether your attention is going to what matters or just to what is in front of you. The tool will handle volume. Deciding where your judgment lands is the part that stays yours.
