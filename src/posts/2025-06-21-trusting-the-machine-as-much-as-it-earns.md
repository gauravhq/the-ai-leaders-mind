---
title: "Trusting the Machine, Exactly as Much as It Earns"
date: 2025-06-21
pageDescription: "Calibrated trust is a dial, not a switch. Earn it task by task."
---
Trust in a tool is not a switch. It is a dial, and the skill is setting it to the right number.

Turn it too high and you get automation bias. You wave through what you should have checked. Turn it too low and the tool is useless, because you re-do by hand everything it hands you. Neither extreme is caution. Both are a refusal to think.

Calibrated trust means matching your reliance to the model's real track record, task by task. It may be excellent at forecasting demand and poor at judging a person. Trusting it evenly across both is not fairness. It is laziness.

So keep score, honestly. Where has this system been reliable, and where has it burned you? Trust the strong areas more and the weak ones less, and be willing to move the dial as the evidence changes. The goal is not blanket faith or blanket doubt. It is a trust that has been earned, one task at a time, and can be lost the same way.
