---
title: "The Future Is a Choice, Not a Forecast"
date: 2025-06-22
pageDescription: "Stop asking what AI will do to us. Start asking what we will do with it."
---
Most conversations about AI and the future are framed as forecasts. What will the tools do to jobs, to judgment, to us? It is the wrong frame, because it casts us as passive, waiting to find out what happens. The more honest frame is a choice. Not what will AI do to us, but what will we do with it.

The forecast frame quietly removes responsibility. If the future is something that happens to you, then your job is only to predict and brace. If it is something you help choose, then your job is to decide well, and that is harder and truer.

Nothing about the outcome is settled. The same tools can deskill people or sharpen them, concentrate power or distribute it, serve people or rule them. Which happens is not a fact waiting to be discovered. It is a choice being made, continuously, by the people using them.

So change the question. Stop asking what the future holds and start asking what you will do to shape it. The forecast frame is a way of avoiding the work. The choice frame is a way of taking it up. The future with these tools is not coming for you. It is being built by you, and everyone like you, in the ongoing choice of how to use what you have been handed.
