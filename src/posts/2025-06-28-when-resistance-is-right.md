---
title: "When Resistance Is Right"
date: 2025-06-28
pageDescription: "Sometimes the person refusing the tool is the only one seeing the flaw."
---
Not all resistance is fear to be managed away. Sometimes the person refusing the tool is the only one in the room who is right.

It is easy, once you have decided AI is the future, to treat every objection as an obstacle. But the nurse who does not trust the triage score, the underwriter who keeps overriding the model, the teacher wary of the grading tool, are sometimes seeing a real flaw that the enthusiasts have talked themselves past. Resistance can be wisdom that has not found the words yet.

A leader who treats all pushback as a problem to crush will win the argument and lose the early warning. The people closest to the work often feel a failure before they can prove it.

So before you override the objection, get curious about it. What does this person see that the rollout plan does not? Sometimes the answer is fear, and you help them through it. Sometimes the answer is a defect in the system, and they just saved you from scaling it. You cannot tell which until you ask.
