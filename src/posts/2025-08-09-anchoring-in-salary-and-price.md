---
title: "Anchoring in Salary and Price"
date: 2025-08-09
pageDescription: "Whoever names the figure first sets the field everyone then argues inside."
---
Watch what happens when a number lands first in a negotiation. An offer, an estimate, a valuation. Everything after it argues in its neighborhood. The first figure becomes the center of gravity, and both sides orbit it without quite deciding to.

This is anchoring, and it does not care whether the number came from a person or a model. When a tool estimates the salary, the deal size, the budget, that figure sets the frame. The conversation quietly shifts from what is right to how far from the anchor anyone is willing to move.

The precise number the machine produces makes it worse, because precision reads as authority, and an authoritative anchor pulls harder.

The defense is to set your own number first, before you look. Decide what you think the figure should be, write it down, and only then let the tool speak. If the two are far apart, that gap is a question worth chasing, not a signal to quietly abandon your figure for the tidier one on the screen. Whoever anchors first has already half won.
