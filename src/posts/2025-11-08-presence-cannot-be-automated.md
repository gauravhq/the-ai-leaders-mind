---
title: "Presence Cannot Be Automated"
date: 2025-11-08
pageDescription: "Being there is the one thing the tool cannot do for you."
---
There is a kind of value that has nothing to do with output, and a machine cannot supply any of it; it is presence. The fact of a person actually being there, attending, carrying the moment with you. No tool can stand in for it, because the whole point is that it is a person.

We forget this because so much of leadership looks like producing things, and the machine is very good at producing things. But a large part of the job was never production. It was showing up, for the hard conversation, the bad news, the moment someone needed to be seen. Automate the production and that part remains, untouched and more important.

You can tell the difference instantly on the receiving end. A message a person wrote and meant lands differently from one a tool generated, even if the words are similar. People know when they are being met and when they are being processed.

So spend your presence deliberately. Let the machine handle what can be produced, and save yourself for the moments that only a present person can hold.
