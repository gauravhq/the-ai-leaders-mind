---
title: "The Question No Dashboard Asks"
date: 2026-01-10
pageDescription: "What is this actually for? The tools will never ask it. You have to."
---
Every dashboard answers questions, and there is one it never asks. What is this actually for? The metrics tell you how things are going against their targets. They cannot tell you whether the targets were the right ones, whether the thing you are optimizing still serves the purpose it was meant to serve. That question is not in any dashboard, because it is not a measurement. It is a judgment, and it is yours.

This is the question that keeps everything else honest. A metric is a proxy for something that matters, and proxies drift. Over time the number becomes the goal, and people optimize it straight past the point where it still means what it was meant to mean. Engagement rises while trust erodes. Efficiency climbs while the work loses its purpose. And no dashboard flags it, because the dashboard is watching the number, not the meaning.

So keep asking the question the tools never will. What is this actually for? Not the proxy on the screen, but the human purpose it was supposed to serve. Are the two still aligned, or have they quietly come apart?

The machines will optimize whatever you point them at, constantly and forever. Staying pointed at what actually matters, rather than at what happens to be measured, is a purely human job. The dashboard will never ask what it is all for. Asking that, and answering it honestly, is one of the things you are still here to do.
