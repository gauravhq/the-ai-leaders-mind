---
title: "Purpose the Metric Cannot Hold"
date: 2026-01-24
pageDescription: "A machine optimizes what you can measure. What is worth measuring stays your job."
---
A machine can optimize almost anything you can measure. What it cannot do is tell you what is worth optimizing for. That question is yours, and it does not survive being handed over.

Give a system a metric and it will pursue it with a focus no human can match, including straight past the point where the metric stops meaning what you thought. Engagement climbs while trust erodes. Efficiency rises while the work loses its soul. The number goes up and something you cared about quietly goes down, because no one told the machine about the something.

This is why purpose has to stay a human job. The model holds the metric; only a person can hold the meaning behind it, the reason the metric existed in the first place, and notice when the two have come apart.

So keep asking the question no dashboard asks. What is this actually for? Not the proxy on the screen, but the human purpose it was meant to serve. The machine will chase the number wherever it leads. Staying pointed at what matters, and not just at what counts, is the part of the job that stays yours.
