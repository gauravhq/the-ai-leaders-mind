---
title: "Culture Is What You Reward"
date: 2026-01-31
pageDescription: "A machine amplifies whatever you actually measure, so measure what you mean."
---
Culture is not what you say in the all-hands; it is what actually gets rewarded, day after day, and people read it accurately no matter what the posters say. Add a machine to the mix and this gets sharper, because a model optimizes exactly what you measure, constantly and at scale.

Tell the tool to maximize a number, and it will, straight past the point where the number stops meaning what you intended. Reward speed and you get speed, including the corners cut to reach it. Reward volume and quality quietly leaves. The machine has no judgment about which of your metrics were meant as proxies and which as goals. It takes them all literally.

So the values you encode become the culture you get, amplified. If what you measure and reward does not match what you actually care about, the gap will grow, because the tool is pulling hard toward the metric while your real intent gets left behind.

The work is to make sure the numbers you reward point at the things you mean. A machine will give you exactly what you asked for. Ask carefully.
