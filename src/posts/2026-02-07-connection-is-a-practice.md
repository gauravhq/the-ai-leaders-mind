---
title: "Connection Is a Practice"
date: 2026-02-07
pageDescription: "Presence is a skill, and like any skill it fades without use."
---
We treat connection as a trait, something you either have or you do not; it is closer to a skill, and skills fade without use. The more you route your relationships through tools, the less you practice the thing itself, and the quieter that capacity gets.

It happens gradually. The tool handles more of the contact, the updates, the check-ins, the small exchanges that used to be face to face. Each handoff is reasonable. The sum is a leader who is more efficient and less practiced at the actual work of being with people, until a moment arrives that needs real presence and the muscle is weak.

The same logic that applies to judgment applies here. What you stop doing, you slowly stop being able to do well. Connection kept alive through use stays strong. Connection outsourced to tools atrophies.

So keep practicing it on purpose. Have the conversations that could have been messages. Be present for the moments a tool could have handled. Not out of nostalgia, but because the ability to genuinely connect is a skill you will need, and the only way to keep it is to keep using it.
