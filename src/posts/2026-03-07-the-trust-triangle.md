title: "The Trust Triangle"
date: 2026-03-07
pageDescription: "Trust in the tool, the people, and yourself. Lean too hard on one corner and it tips."
---
Most conversations about AI and trust ask one question. Do you trust the machine? That is only one side of a triangle.

There are three trusts in the room, not one. Trust in the tool, trust in the people who use it, and trust in yourself. Lean too hard on any single corner and the whole thing tips. Trust the machine and not your people, and you manage a fleet of button-pushers. Trust yourself and not the machine, and you throw away real help. Trust the people and never yourself, and no one is actually steering.

Healthy teams hold all three in tension. The tool earns trust by being reliable and honest about its limits; the people earn it by using the tool with judgment. And you earn your own by staying in the decisions that matter instead of quietly outsourcing them.

When something goes wrong with AI in a team, it is rarely just the model. It is usually the triangle out of balance. Ask which corner you have been over-trusting lately. The answer is often the whole problem.
