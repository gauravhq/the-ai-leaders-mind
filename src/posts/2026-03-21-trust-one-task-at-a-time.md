---
title: "Trust One Task at a Time"
date: 2026-03-21
pageDescription: "A tool is not trustworthy or untrustworthy. It earns your reliance task by task."
---
We want a single verdict on a tool. Is it good or not, safe or not, to be trusted or not. The honest answer is almost always that it depends on the task, and forcing a single verdict is where calibration goes wrong.

A model can be excellent at forecasting demand and poor at judging a person. Reliable on the routine and shaky on the rare. If you trust it evenly across all of that, you are wrong in two directions at once, over-relying where it is weak and under-using where it is strong.

So keep the trust local. Ask not whether you trust the tool, but whether you trust it for this, right now, on this kind of call. The answer will differ across the day, and that variation is not indecision; it is accuracy.

This is more work than a blanket rule, which is exactly why people avoid it. But a blanket rule is a blanket error. Trust granted one task at a time is the only kind that fits how a tool actually performs.
