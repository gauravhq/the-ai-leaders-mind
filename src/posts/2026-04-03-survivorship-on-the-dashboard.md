---
title: "Survivorship on the Dashboard"
date: 2026-04-03
pageDescription: "The metric shows you who stayed. It says nothing about who left."
---
A dashboard has a blind spot built into it. It shows you the people who are still there. The customers who churned, the employees who quit, the users who gave up, they are gone, and mostly they are gone from the data too. You are looking at the survivors.

This quietly distorts every read. Satisfaction looks high because the unhappy ones already left. A process looks smooth because the people it failed are no longer in the system to register the friction. The picture is real, and it is missing exactly the part that would tell you what is going wrong.

A model built on that data inherits the blind spot and hands it back with confidence.

So keep asking who is not in the picture. Where did the people who left go, and why? What would the customer too frustrated to answer the survey have said? The most important signal is often the absence, the silence where a dissatisfied person used to be. A dashboard cannot show you who is gone. Remembering to ask is your job.
