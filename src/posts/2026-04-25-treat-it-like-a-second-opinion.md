---
title: "Treat It Like a Second Opinion"
date: 2026-04-25
pageDescription: "Not an oracle to obey, a fast colleague to interrogate."
---
There is a useful stance toward a capable model, and it sits between two bad ones. On one side, the oracle you obey. On the other, the toy you dismiss. Neither gets the value.

The stance that works is the second opinion. You would not let a consultant make your decision, and you would not ignore one either. You would listen, push back, ask how they reached it, and weigh it against your own read. A model deserves exactly that.

Treated this way, its confidence stops being a problem. A second opinion is allowed to be wrong. You are not looking for something to defer to. You are looking for another angle to test your thinking against.

This also keeps you in the work. A person who interrogates an answer stays a decision-maker. A person who accepts one becomes a clerk. The tool can be the most useful colleague you have ever had, fast, tireless, widely read. It just should never be the one who signs.
