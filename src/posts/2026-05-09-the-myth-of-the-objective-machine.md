---
title: "The Myth of the Objective Machine"
date: 2026-05-09
pageDescription: "A model is not neutral. It carries the values baked into its data."
---
It is tempting to reach for the machine when a decision feels political or fraught, because it seems to offer a way out of human bias. The number is just the number. The model does not have a horse in the race.

That comfort is false. A model learns from data, and data is a record of human choices, with all their patterns and blind spots inside it. What looks like objectivity is often just bias with the fingerprints wiped off, harder to see because it arrives as math.

So the machine does not remove values from a decision. It hides where they came from. The judgment about what to optimize, whose outcomes count, and what to ignore is still there. It has just moved out of sight.

The honest move is to keep asking what the model was taught to want and whose reality shaped it. Use it for what it is good at, but never as a way to launder a hard human choice into a clean technical one.
