---
title: "Beyond What the Machine Can See"
date: 2026-05-29
pageDescription: "Every model works from a window, and every window has a frame."
---
Every model works from what it was given. The data is a window, and every window has a frame. What falls outside the frame does not exist as far as the machine is concerned.

The person the numbers describe is always more than the numbers. The high performer whose home is falling apart. The quiet team that is one resignation from collapse. The customer whose last three orders were a cry for help the sentiment score read as neutral. The model sees the trace. It does not see the life.

Leaders get into trouble when they mistake the window for the world. If it is not in the data, it did not happen. That is a comfortable story and a false one.

The fix is not to distrust the data. It is to keep asking what the frame leaves out. Who is not in this dataset. What does this metric fail to capture. What would I know if I walked over and asked. The machine narrows the view to what it can measure. Your job is to keep widening it.
