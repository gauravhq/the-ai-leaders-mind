---
title: "The View From Above"
date: 2026-06-06
pageDescription: "Zoom out far enough and today's panic looks like one step in a long human pattern."
---
The Stoics had a practice of imagining the present from a great height, or a great distance in time, to cut it down to size. Applied now, it is oddly steadying. Zoom out far enough, and the current panic about AI looks like one more instance of a very old human pattern, a powerful new tool arriving faster than the wisdom to use it.

This is not to dismiss what is happening. It is real and it matters. But the feeling that this moment is uniquely overwhelming, that no one has ever faced anything like it, is partly a trick of being inside it. People have met the printing press, the factory, the computer, each time convinced the ground was ending, each time finding a way to lead through.

The view from above does not make the work easier. It makes the panic smaller, which frees you to do the work. Fear shrinks your thinking to the next threat. Distance restores the longer view, where you can see that adaptation is possible because it has happened before, many times, by people no more equipped than you.

So when the moment feels too big, step back from it on purpose. The problem stays real. The paralysis eases. And from a little height, you can usually see the next practical step you could not find while standing too close.
