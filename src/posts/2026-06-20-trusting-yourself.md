---
title: "Trusting Yourself"
date: 2026-06-20
pageDescription: "Decide before you look, so there is still a you in the loop worth correcting."
---
The quietest casualty of a very capable machine is your faith in your own judgment.

It creeps in. The model is right often enough that second-guessing it starts to feel foolish. So you stop forming your own view first. You wait to see what the screen says, then agree. Over time you are not sure you still have a read of your own, and that uncertainty is its own kind of risk, because the day the tool fails, your judgment is what is left.

Trusting yourself does not mean overruling the machine to prove you can. That is ego, not judgment. It means keeping your own opinion alive alongside the model's, so the two can actually be compared.

The practice is simple and easy to skip. Decide before you look. Form a rough view, then open the tool. Some days it sharpens you and some days it corrects you, and both are fine. What matters is that there was a you in the loop, with a view worth correcting.
