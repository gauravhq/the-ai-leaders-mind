---
title: "The Dashboard-Goes-Dark Drill"
date: 2026-07-04
pageDescription: "Practice deciding without the tools, so you can when you have to."
---
There is a simple exercise worth doing before you need it. Once in a while, make a real decision as if the tools were unavailable. No model, no dashboard, no automated recommendation. Just your own judgment, the way it would have to work if the systems were down.

The aim is not to prove you do not need the tools. You do, and pretending otherwise is silly. It is to keep the underlying capability alive, so that when the dashboard actually goes dark, and eventually it will, you are not helpless in front of a decision you have always let the machine shape.

It is a drill, like any other. Pilots practice flying without instruments not because they plan to, but because the day the instruments fail is not the day to learn. Your judgment deserves the same treatment. Exercise it in calm conditions so it is there in the emergency.

So schedule the occasional unaided call. Feel how rusty or sharp your own thinking is. The rust you find is the warning, the places you have leaned so hard on the tools that you could no longer function without them. Better to discover that in a drill than in the outage, when the stakes are real and the machine is gone.
