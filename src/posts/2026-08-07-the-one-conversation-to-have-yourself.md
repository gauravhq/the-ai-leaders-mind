---
title: "The One Conversation to Have Yourself"
date: 2026-08-07
pageDescription: "Delegate the logistics, but keep the human moment for yourself."
---
In any week there is usually one conversation that you should not delegate, automate, or route around, and the skill is knowing which one it is. The tools can carry an enormous amount of your communication. There is always a small remainder that has to be you, in person, unmediated.

It is the conversation where trust is on the line. Where someone needs to hear a hard thing from you directly, or where your presence is the whole message. Automate that one and you have not saved time. You have quietly told the person they did not warrant your actual attention.

The rest can flow through tools, and should. Most communication is logistics, and machines handle logistics well. The art is protecting the exception, the single interaction that carries more weight than all the others, and giving it your undivided, unautomated self.

So each week, find the one conversation that has to be yours. Let the tools take everything around it. Then show up for that one fully, because it is the interaction people will remember, and the one where being a present human is the entire point.
