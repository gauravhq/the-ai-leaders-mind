---
title: "The Stoic Morning Question"
date: 2026-08-15
pageDescription: "Start the day by sorting what is yours to control from what is not."
---
The Stoics began the day by preparing the mind, and one small version of that practice fits a working life beside machines almost perfectly. Before the day starts, ask what is actually in your control today and what is not, and decide to spend your energy on the first.

The forces around you, the pace of the technology, the decisions being made above you, the general uncertainty, will press for your attention and repay none of it. A minute spent sorting them from the things you can actually affect, your effort, your judgment, your attention, how you treat people, sets the day on better ground.

It is a small practice with an outsized effect, because most wasted worry comes from energy poured into what you cannot change, and most real progress comes from energy focused on what you can. The morning question quietly aims you at the second.

So try it. Before the tools and the messages and the demands begin, take the moment to name what is yours to control today. It does not make the uncontrollable go away. It stops you from spending yourself against it, and leaves more of you for the work that is actually in your hands.
