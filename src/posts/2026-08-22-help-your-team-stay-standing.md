---
title: "Help Your Team Stay Standing"
date: 2026-08-22
pageDescription: "Your resilience matters most when it becomes something your people can lean on."
---
Personal resilience is good. Resilience you can extend to a team is leadership. The steadiness you build in yourself is worth more when it becomes something your people can lean on, because a team facing hard change needs stable ground more than it needs another anxious person, however senior.

You help a team stay standing in concrete ways. You give them a clear sense of what stays theirs when the tools take the rest. You share honest information instead of leaving them to fill the silence with fear. You keep them practicing their own judgment so their confidence does not depend entirely on the systems. And you stay steady enough yourself that there is something for them to draw on.

None of this is soft. A team that stays standing through change keeps thinking, keeps deciding well, keeps functioning when others freeze. A team that comes apart under the pressure loses exactly the judgment it needs most.

So treat your people's resilience as part of your job, not just your own. Build the ground they can stand on. In a period where the tools will keep shifting under everyone, a leader who helps their team stay upright through it is doing something no machine can do, and something the team will remember long after this particular change is old news.
