date: 2026-08-23

---
For all the talk of algorithms and models, the hardest problem in AI leadership is the oldest one there is. It is people.

The technology is new. The human responses to it are ancient. Fear of change, the pull of the easy answer, the wish to hand a hard call to someone or something else, the way trust is slow to build and quick to break. None of that is a computing problem. It is the same human material leaders have always worked with, wearing new clothes.

This is oddly good news. It means the deepest skills of leadership did not expire when the machines got smart. Reading a room, naming a fear, holding steady, keeping trust, deciding what you can live with. The tools raise the stakes on all of it and replace none of it.

So do not let the novelty fool you into thinking the work itself changed. The interface changed. The work is still about people, their fears and their trust and their willingness to follow you into something uncertain. Get the machine right and the people wrong, and you have solved nothing.
