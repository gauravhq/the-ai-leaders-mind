---
title: "Confirmation Bias in Strategy"
date: 2026-09-05
pageDescription: "The model can dress up the strategy you already chose in the language of analysis."
---
Strategy is where confirmation bias is most expensive, because the decisions are large and the feedback is slow. You form a view early, often for reasons you cannot fully see, and then you look for support. A capable model is a dangerous friend here, because it can supply support for almost anything.

Bring it your favored direction and ask for the case, and it builds a strong one. The analysis looks rigorous, the logic holds, and you walk out more sure than you walked in, having learned nothing except that the tool agrees with the thing you brought it.

That is not analysis; it is a mirror with good vocabulary.

The way to use the machine in strategy is to make it argue the roads you did not take. Ask it to build the strongest case for the opposite direction. Ask what would have to be true for your favored plan to be a mistake. If the alternative case is weak, you have learned something real. If it is strong, you just avoided walking confidently off a cliff your own bias had hidden.
