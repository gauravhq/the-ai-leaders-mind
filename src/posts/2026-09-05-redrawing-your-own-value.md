---
title: "Redrawing Your Own Value"
date: 2026-09-05
pageDescription: "Stop competing with the machine at what it does best. Move your worth to what needs a person."
---
When a machine can do a large part of what made you good at your job, the fear underneath is simple. If the tool does that, what am I for?

It is a real question, and the wrong answer is to compete with the machine at the thing the machine is better at. You will lose that race, and exhaust yourself losing it. The work is to redraw the line around what is actually yours.

Much of what felt like the core of your job was mechanics. The analysis, the first draft, the pattern spotted in the numbers. The machine is taking the mechanics. What it cannot take is judgment under uncertainty, responsibility for the call, the trust you hold with real people, and the meaning you help them find in the work.

That is not a consolation prize. It is the harder, more human part of the job, the part that was always underpaid attention. Redrawing your value means moving your sense of worth from the tasks a model can absorb to the ones that need a person. Do that, and the tool stops being a threat and starts working for you.
