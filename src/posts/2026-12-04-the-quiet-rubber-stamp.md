---
title: "The Quiet Rubber-Stamp"
date: 2026-12-04
pageDescription: "Approval without thought is the most common way a tool takes over a decision."
---
There is a failure that leaves no trace and does enormous damage; it is the quiet rubber-stamp, the approval given without real thought, the yes that looks like a decision and was actually a reflex. It is how tools take over decisions without anyone deciding to let them.

It feels like work. You looked at the output, you clicked approve, you moved on. But if you would have approved anything the tool produced, you did not review it. You processed it. The decision was made by the machine, and you supplied a signature.

The tell is whether you could have said no. If nothing the model showed you would have changed your action, you were never really in the loop. The review was theater.

So test yourself now and then on the calls you wave through. What would the output have to say for me to reject it? If you cannot answer, you are rubber-stamping, and it is worth catching, because a stamp given a hundred times becomes a habit, and the habit is what fails you on the day the output was wrong.
