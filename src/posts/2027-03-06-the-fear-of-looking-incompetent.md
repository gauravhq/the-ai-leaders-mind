---
title: "The Fear of Looking Incompetent"
date: 2027-03-06
pageDescription: "Being a beginner again is humiliating for people who were good at their jobs."
---
One of the quieter fears about new tools is not about the tool at all. It is about looking foolish. Someone who was genuinely skilled, respected for knowing their work, is suddenly a fumbling beginner in front of a machine everyone else seems to have figured out. That is humiliating, and humiliation makes people avoid the thing that causes it.

This fear hides as disinterest or stubbornness. The person who will not engage with the tool may not be against it. They may be protecting themselves from the exposure of being bad at something in public, after years of being good.

Knowing this changes how you help. You do not push harder or make the case louder. You make it safe to be a beginner. You normalize the fumbling, share your own, and remove the audience from the early clumsy attempts.

Competent people fear incompetence more than most, because they have more to lose in status and self-image. Give them room to learn without being watched, and the resistance often dissolves, because it was never about the tool. It was about not wanting to look diminished in front of people who used to look up to them.
