---
title: "The Fear of a Future You Cannot Picture"
date: 2027-04-02
pageDescription: "People do not fear change so much as a change they cannot see themselves inside."
---
People are often described as afraid of change, but that is not quite it. They are afraid of a future they cannot picture themselves in. Change toward something they can imagine and want is exciting. Change toward a blank, or toward a picture that does not include them, is frightening. The tools often present the second kind.

When AI arrives and no one paints a clear picture of what work looks like on the other side, people fill the blank with their fears. They imagine the version where they are unnecessary, because that is what an unlit future tends to look like from inside anxiety.

So a large part of leading change is supplying the picture. Not a fake, glossy one, but an honest, concrete image of what the team does after the tools settle in, and where each person fits. People can move toward a future they can see themselves inside. They dig in against a void.

The fear of an unpicturable future is answered by making it picturable. Describe the work on the other side. Put the person in the picture. A future someone can imagine themselves in stops being a threat and starts being a place they can choose to walk toward.
