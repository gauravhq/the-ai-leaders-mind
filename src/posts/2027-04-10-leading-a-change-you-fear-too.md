---
title: "Leading a Change You Fear Too"
date: 2027-04-10
pageDescription: "You do not have to be fearless. You have to be a step ahead, honestly."
---
There is a myth that to lead people through a change, you must be untroubled by it yourself. It is not true, and trying to fake it usually backfires. You can lead people through a change you also find frightening. You just have to be a step ahead of them, and honest about the walk.

Faked confidence is thin, and people can feel through it. A leader who pretends the AI transition is nothing but opportunity, while obviously uneasy underneath, teaches the team to distrust what they are told. Honesty about the difficulty, paired with steadiness about the direction, is far more credible and far more calming.

Being a step ahead does not mean having all the answers. It means having faced the fear a little sooner, found some footing, and being willing to bring others along the same path you are still walking yourself.

So do not wait to feel fearless before you lead. You will wait forever. Acknowledge the fear, keep moving through it, and let people see that moving through it is possible, because you are doing it in front of them. That is more useful than confidence, and unlike confidence, it is true.
