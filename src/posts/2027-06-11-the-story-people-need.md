---
title: "The Story People Need"
date: 2027-06-11
pageDescription: "Facts inform. A story is what lets people locate themselves in a change."
---
People do not adopt a change because the facts favor it. They adopt it when they can locate themselves in a story about where things are going. Facts inform, but a story is what lets someone see their place in what is coming, and it is the place, not the facts, that people are really deciding about.

When AI arrives with only data behind it, people fill the narrative gap themselves, and the story they tell is usually the frightening one, the version where they are made unnecessary. Left without a story, fear writes one.

So a leader has to offer a story, an honest one. Not a fairy tale about how everything will be wonderful, but a believable account of where the team is heading, why, and how each person fits into it. A story people can see themselves inside is what turns a threatening change into a shared direction.

This is not manipulation if it is true. It is the difference between handing people a pile of facts and handing them a way to make sense of the facts. People move toward a future they can narrate. Give them one worth walking into, and mean it.
