---
title: "The Choices We Face"
date: 2027-06-26
pageDescription: "The important decisions about AI are not technical. They are about what we value."
---
The loudest questions about AI are technical, about what the tools can do and how fast they will improve. The questions that actually decide the future are not technical at all. They are about what we choose to value, and they will be answered by leaders, one decision at a time, whether or not they notice they are answering them.

Do we use these tools to replace people or to make people more capable? Do we let them concentrate judgment in a few hands or spread it? Do we optimize for what is easy to measure or for what actually matters? None of these have technical answers. They are choices about values, and the technology does not make them. People do.

This is the part that gets lost in the excitement about capability. The tools are powerful and getting more so, and that only raises the stakes on the human choices about how to use them. Power without a choice about its direction just amplifies whatever we already were.

So take the choices seriously as choices. The future is not being decided in the labs alone. It is being decided in the countless ordinary decisions leaders make about how to point these tools, and those decisions are about values, which means they are yours to make well or badly.
