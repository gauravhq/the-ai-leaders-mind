---
title: "Help the Expert Who Feels Obsolete"
date: 2027-07-02
pageDescription: "The person whose skill the machine now mimics needs their value redrawn, not defended."
---
The hardest person to lead through an AI transition is often your best one. The deep expert, the person whose skill the machine now imitates, faces a specific and painful version of the change, because the thing that made them valuable is the thing being automated.

Their instinct, and sometimes yours, is to defend the old skill, to argue it is still better, still needed, still theirs. Sometimes that is true and worth saying. Often it is a losing fight, and fighting it keeps the person anchored to a value that is genuinely eroding.

The better help is to redraw their value rather than defend it. The specific task may be automatable. The judgment, the experience, the sense of what matters that they built underneath the task usually is not, and it is more valuable now, not less, because it is what tells you when the machine is wrong.

So help your experts move their sense of worth from the skill the tool copies to the deeper judgment it cannot. That is not a demotion. It is a promotion, from doing the work to knowing when the work is right, and the people best placed to make that move are exactly the ones who mastered it in the first place.
