---
title: "Technology Serves People, Not the Reverse"
date: 2027-07-03
pageDescription: "The simplest principle, and the easiest to quietly invert."
---
There is a principle simple enough to state in a sentence and easy enough to invert without noticing. Technology should serve people, not the other way around. It sounds obvious, and organizations reverse it constantly, ending up with people serving the system, contorting themselves to feed the tool the inputs it wants and chase the metrics it optimizes.

The inversion happens quietly. A tool is introduced to help, and slowly the help becomes the master. People start shaping their work around what the system measures, spending their days feeding the machine rather than being served by it. No one decided this. It crept in, one accommodation at a time.

Catching it requires asking the question directly and often. Is this tool serving the people, or are the people serving it? Does it make the work more human or less? Is it freeing attention for what matters or consuming attention with its own demands?

So keep the principle in front of you, because the drift is always toward inversion. Technology that serves people is the goal, and it does not maintain itself. Left alone, systems tend to make people serve them. Keeping the arrow pointed the right way is a choice you have to keep making, because nothing about the tools will make it for you.
