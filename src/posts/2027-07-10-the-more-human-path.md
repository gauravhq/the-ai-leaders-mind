---
title: "The More Human Path"
date: 2027-07-10
pageDescription: "Used well, AI can free leaders to be more human, not less."
---
There is a version of the future where AI makes leaders less human, where they hide behind the tools, outsource the hard conversations, and slowly lose the judgment and presence that made them leaders. It is a real risk, and it is not the only path.

There is another version, where the tools take the mechanical work and free the leader to be more human, not less. The machine handles the analysis, the drafting, the routine, and the time and attention that frees goes toward the things only a person can do. The hard conversation you now have the space to have well. The judgment you can bring more fully because you are not buried in production. The presence you can offer because the busywork is handled.

Which version you get is not decided by the technology. It is decided by what you do with what the tools free up. The same efficiency can be spent hiding from people or showing up for them more fully.

So choose the more human path deliberately. Let the machine take the mechanical, and reinvest the freed attention in the human core of the work, the deciding, the trusting, the being present. Used this way, the tools do not diminish you. They clear space for the parts of leadership that were always the point, and never something a machine could do.
