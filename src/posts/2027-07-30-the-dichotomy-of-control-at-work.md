---
title: "The Dichotomy of Control at Work"
date: 2027-07-30
pageDescription: "Spend energy on what you can change. Let the rest go, on purpose."
---
The Stoics offered one idea that fits this moment almost too well. Divide the world into what you control and what you do not, and spend your energy only on the first. Applied to working with AI, it is a practical tool, not just a philosophy.

You do not control how fast the technology moves, what the tools become, or whether your whole field is about to be reshaped. Pour worry into those and you drown, because none of them answer to your worry. They will do what they do regardless of how much sleep you lose.

What you control is smaller and more useful. Which tools you learn. How carefully you check the ones you use. The judgment you bring. Whether you keep your own skills sharp. How you treat the people around you as the ground shifts.

The discipline is to keep pulling your attention back from the first list to the second. Not because the big forces do not matter, but because your energy does nothing there and everything here. In a moment this uncertain, aiming your effort where it can actually work is not resignation. It is the most practical thing you can do.
