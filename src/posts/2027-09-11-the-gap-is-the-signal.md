---
title: "The Gap Is the Signal"
date: 2027-09-11
pageDescription: "Where your read and the model's disagree is where the real thinking happens."
---
When you have formed your own view and the model has offered its own, the most valuable thing in the decision is the gap between them. Where you and the machine agree, there is not much to do. Where you disagree is where the real thinking lives, and it is the part most people rush past.

The disagreement is a signal, not a nuisance. It means one of you is seeing something the other is missing. Maybe the model has data or a pattern you overlooked. Maybe you have context, judgment, or a sense of the situation that the model could never access. Either way, the gap is pointing at exactly the place that deserves attention, and resolving it well is where good decisions are made.

The temptation is to close the gap fast, usually by deferring to the tool, because sitting in disagreement is uncomfortable. That comfort-seeking throws away the signal.

So when your read and the model's diverge, slow down there. Ask what the machine might be seeing that you are not, and what you might be seeing that it cannot. The gap is not a problem to eliminate. It is the decision telling you where to look, and following it is often the difference between a call you thought about and one you rushed.
