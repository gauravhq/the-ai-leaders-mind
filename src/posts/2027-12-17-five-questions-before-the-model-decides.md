---
title: "Five Questions Before You Let the Model Decide"
date: 2027-12-17
pageDescription: "A speed bump you place on purpose, right where the road is most dangerous."
---
When a decision carries real weight for real people, the tool should not get the last word without you asking a few things first. Five questions do most of the work.

Who is affected, and who among them cannot push back? What does the model optimize for, and what does that quietly ignore? Where does its data come from, and whose reality is missing from it? What happens to the person it gets wrong? And could I explain this decision, out loud, to the human on the other end of it?

None of these require a philosophy degree. They require a pause, and the willingness to sit with an answer you may not like.

The aim is not to slow every decision to a crawl. Most calls do not need this. But the ones that shape a person's livelihood, freedom, or dignity do, and those are exactly the ones a fast, confident system will try to wave through. The five questions are a speed bump you place on purpose, right where the road is most dangerous. Ask them, and you stay the one deciding.
