title: "Reflective Awareness Is the Foundation"
date: 2027-12-25
pageDescription: "You cannot correct a bias you cannot see in yourself as it happens."
---
Of all the capacities that will matter as machines take on more of the work, one sits underneath the rest. The ability to watch your own thinking while you think.

Reflective awareness is noticing that you are anchoring before the anchor sets. Catching the small relief of deferring to the model. Feeling the pull to agree with the confident answer and pausing on it. Without this, every other skill is theoretical, because you cannot correct a bias you cannot see in yourself in the moment it is happening.

The machine makes this both harder and more important. Harder, because a fluent answer invites you to stop thinking. More important, because the errors are subtle and self-flattering, and nothing on the screen will flag them for you.

You build the capacity the way you build any other, through reps. A minute at the end of a decision, asking how you actually reached it. A habit of naming the reflex as it fires. It feels slow and slightly awkward. It is also the difference between using the tool and being quietly steered by it.
