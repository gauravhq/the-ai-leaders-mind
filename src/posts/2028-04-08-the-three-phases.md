title: "The Three Phases"
date: 2028-04-08
pageDescription: "Most leaders think they are in phase one while already living in phase two."
---
AI did not arrive in leadership all at once. It came, and is still coming, in three phases, and knowing which one you are in changes what your job is.

In the first phase, the machine assists. It speeds up tasks you already do, and you stay firmly in charge. In the second, it augments. It starts shaping the options you see and the way you frame the problem, and your judgment and its output begin to blend. In the third, it becomes symbiotic. The work is genuinely shared, and it gets hard to say where the machine's thinking ends and yours begins.

Most leaders think they are in phase one while they are already living in phase two. The model is not just saving time. It is quietly setting the terms of the decision, and no one noticed the line move.

The value of naming the phases is honesty. You cannot manage an influence you will not admit is there. Ask which phase you are actually in with a given tool. The answer is usually one step further than comfortable, and that is the step worth minding.
