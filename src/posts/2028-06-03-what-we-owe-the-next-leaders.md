---
title: "What We Owe the Next Leaders"
date: 2028-06-03
pageDescription: "How we use these tools now shapes what leadership becomes for the people after us."
---
The way this generation of leaders uses AI will shape what leadership becomes for the people who come after. That is a responsibility worth feeling. The norms being set now, about what gets automated, what stays human, how much judgment people keep, are not just about today. They are the inheritance the next leaders will step into.

If we build a world where leaders defer to the tools, hide behind them, and let their judgment atrophy, that becomes the normal that the next generation learns as the only way. If we build one where the tools amplify human judgment rather than replacing it, where people stay capable and present alongside the machines, that becomes their inheritance instead.

This is easy to lose sight of in the pressure of the moment, where each decision feels local and immediate. But the accumulation of those decisions is setting a pattern that will outlast the people making it.

So lead now with the next leaders in mind. Use these tools in the way you would want to become the norm, the way you would want the people after you to inherit. What we practice becomes what they assume. We owe them a version of leadership beside machines that keeps the human part central, and we build it, or fail to, in how we lead today.
