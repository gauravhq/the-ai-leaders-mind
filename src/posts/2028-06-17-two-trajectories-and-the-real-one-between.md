---
title: "Two Trajectories, and the Real One Between"
date: 2028-06-17
pageDescription: "The future with AI is neither the utopia nor the collapse. It is the one we choose."
---
There are two easy stories about AI and the future, and both are a way of avoiding responsibility. One is the utopia, where the tools solve everything and we relax into abundance. The other is the collapse, where they take the jobs, the judgment, and the meaning, and we are left diminished. Both are told as if they will simply happen to us.

The real trajectory is neither, and it is not a forecast. It is the sum of choices, yours among them, about how these tools get used. The utopia and the collapse are both fantasies of passivity, futures that arrive on their own. The actual future is built decision by decision, by people deciding whether the machine sharpens them or replaces them.

This is harder than either story, because it puts the outcome partly in your hands. You do not get to wait and see which future shows up. You help make it, in how you lead, decide, and treat people beside the tools.

So resist both easy stories. The future with AI is not a thing to predict. It is a thing to build, and the version we get depends on choices being made right now, by people like you, whether or not they realize they are making it.
