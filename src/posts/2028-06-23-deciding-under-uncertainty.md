---
title: "Deciding Under Uncertainty"
date: 2028-06-23
pageDescription: "The tool sounds certain. The situation is not. Decide anyway, with your eyes open."
---
Most real decisions are made under uncertainty, and a machine has a strange effect on that. It removes the feeling of uncertainty without removing the uncertainty itself. The answer arrives clean and confident, and the genuine not-knowing that should be part of the decision quietly disappears from view.

This is dangerous because good judgment under uncertainty depends on staying honest about what you do not know. The tool's false clarity tempts you to act as if the situation were more settled than it is, to treat a confident output as if it resolved the doubt it merely papered over.

So keep the uncertainty in view even when the machine hides it. Ask what the answer depends on, what would change it, how wrong it could be. Decide anyway, because waiting for certainty means never deciding, but decide with your eyes open to the real range of outcomes rather than the single clean number on the screen.

Deciding well under uncertainty is not about eliminating doubt. It is about holding it honestly while still acting, hedging where you can, staying ready to adjust. The tool will offer you the comfort of false certainty. Take the answer, keep the honesty, and make the call knowing what you do not know, because that is what the situation actually requires.
