---
title: "Optimism With Clear Eyes"
date: 2028-07-22
pageDescription: "You can believe the future can be good without pretending the risks are not real."
---
There is a false choice between optimism and honesty about AI, as if you have to pick between believing the future can be good and taking the risks seriously. The most useful stance holds both. Optimism with clear eyes. A belief that these tools can make things better, paired with an unflinching look at the ways they could make things worse.

Pure optimism is a liability, because it looks away from the real dangers, the deskilling, the concentrated harm, the erosion of judgment, and so does nothing to prevent them. Pure pessimism is also a liability, because it treats the bad outcomes as inevitable and gives up the agency to steer toward better ones.

Clear-eyed optimism is the working stance. It says the good future is possible and not guaranteed, that it has to be built and defended, and that seeing the risks clearly is part of building it, not a betrayal of hope.

So hold both. Believe these tools can serve people and human flourishing, and take seriously every way they might not. The belief keeps you working toward the good outcome. The clear eyes keep you honest about what stands in its way. Neither alone is enough. Together they are how you actually help the better future come true, rather than just hoping for it or bracing for its opposite.
