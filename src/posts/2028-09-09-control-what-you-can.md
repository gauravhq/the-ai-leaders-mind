title: "Control What You Can"
date: 2028-09-09
pageDescription: "The Stoic move is to aim your effort where it can actually do something."
---
Two thousand years ago the Stoics offered a tool that turns out to be built for this moment. Divide the world into what you control and what you do not, and spend your energy only on the first.

Working with AI throws that line into sharp relief. You do not control what the model does, how fast the technology moves, or whether your industry is about to be reshaped. Pour your worry into those and you will drown, because none of it answers to your worry.

What you do control is smaller and more powerful. Which tools you learn. How carefully you check the ones you use. The judgment you bring to the call. Whether you keep your own skills sharp. The way you treat the people around you as the ground shifts.

The Stoic move is not calm for its own sake. It is a way to aim your effort where it can actually do something. Fear scatters attention across things you cannot change; this puts it back on the few things you can. In a moment this uncertain, that focus is not a luxury. It is the whole discipline.
