---
title: "The Beginner You Avoid Being"
date: 2028-10-28
pageDescription: "The discomfort you are dodging is the exact place your growth is waiting."
---
There is probably a tool, a skill, or a way of working that you have been avoiding, not because it is useless but because engaging with it would make you a beginner again, and being a beginner is uncomfortable when you are used to being competent. That avoidance is worth noticing, because the discomfort you are dodging is usually the exact place your growth is waiting.

It is a natural thing to do. Once you are good at your work, the prospect of being visibly bad at something new feels like a threat to your standing and your sense of yourself. So you find reasons to stay in the zone of your existing competence, and the reasons feel legitimate, and meanwhile the world keeps moving toward the thing you are avoiding.

The people who keep growing are the ones who push into that discomfort on purpose, who let themselves be beginners repeatedly rather than clinging to being experts at something aging.

So find the beginner you have been avoiding being. What are you steering around because it would make you fumble? That is likely where the growth is, precisely because you have been protecting yourself from it. The willingness to be bad at something new, again, is what keeps you learning, and the thing you most avoid being a beginner at is often the thing you most need to start.
