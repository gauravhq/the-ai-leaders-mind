title: "Your Role Is Not Small"
date: 2029-02-02
pageDescription: "The future of leadership beside AI is decided by individual leaders, one at a time."
---
It is easy to feel small against something as large as AI, to assume the future is being decided by tech companies and researchers and forces far above your pay grade, and that your own choices barely register. That feeling is understandable and mostly wrong. The future of leadership beside these tools is decided by individual leaders, one at a time, and you are one of them.

The big forces set the possibilities. What actually happens inside them is determined by countless ordinary leaders deciding how to use the tools with their own teams. Whether people stay capable or grow dependent, whether decisions keep human owners, whether the technology serves the people, these are settled not in the labs but in the daily choices of leaders like you.

So your role is not small, even if it feels that way. The aggregate future is just the sum of individual leaders getting it right or wrong, and your piece of that sum is entirely yours.

You will not change the trajectory of the technology. You will completely determine how it plays out in your corner of it, for your people, in your decisions. That is not small. It is the level at which the future actually gets built, one leader and one team at a time, and it is exactly the level you operate on.
