---
title: "The Day the Tool Was Wrong"
date: 2029-04-20
pageDescription: "Every reliable system has one. What matters is whether you were still watching."
---
Every tool you rely on will have a day when it is badly wrong. Not a little off, but confidently, consequentially wrong, in a way that matters. This is not a flaw to be surprised by. It is a certainty to prepare for, because no system is right every time, and the only question is whether you will be watching when its day comes.

The trouble is that reliability breeds inattention. A tool that has been right a hundred times trains you to stop checking, so that on the hundred and first time, when it is wrong, no one is looking. The very track record that earned your trust is what set up the failure, because trust quietly became autopilot.

So the preparation is to keep some attention alive even on your most reliable tools, especially on them. Not constant suspicion, which is exhausting and wasteful, but a maintained habit of checking the outputs you would otherwise wave through, precisely because you would otherwise wave them through.

The day the tool is wrong is coming, for every tool, eventually. Whether it becomes a caught error or a disaster depends entirely on whether a person was still paying attention when it arrived. Reliability is not a reason to stop watching. It is the thing that makes stopping feel safe, right up until the day it is not.
